Enclave is an elite, self-hosted, zero-knowledge private communication platform built specifically for couples navigating long-distance relationships who want absolute control over their digital intimacy.

Unlike commercial messaging apps, Enclave removes third-party cloud infrastructure entirely. Your conversations, shared moments, photos, and voice notes are encrypted end-to-end and stored exclusively on your own server — never on a third-party cloud. Not even the developers can read your data.

KEY FEATURES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• CRYPTOGRAPHIC SOVEREIGNTY
Industry-standard E2EE powered by the Signal Protocol (Double Ratchet algorithm + X3DH key agreement). Every message, photo, and voice note is encrypted before it leaves your device. Local data at rest is protected by hardware-backed AES-256 encryption and biometric locks.

• ZERO-KNOWLEDGE BACKUPS
Encrypted backups are written directly to your self-hosted instance. The backup keys never leave your devices. Not even your VPS provider or server administrator can inspect the contents of your data.

• THE KISS SIMULATOR
A signature multi-sensory interaction space featuring real-time soft-body physics simulation, mutual touch synchronization over WebRTC, haptic feedback, and spatial ASMR audio synthesis. Designed to bridge the physical gap of long-distance relationships.

• LIVE COLLABORATIVE CANVAS
A real-time shared whiteboard synchronized over low-latency WebSockets. Draw, sketch, and annotate together from opposite sides of the world.

• THE LOUNGE
A shared intimate environment containing: a collaborative photo scrapbook, shared countdown timers to your next meeting, a synchronized music player, status stories, and daily encrypted love letters.

• SECRET PHOTOS & TIMELINE
A private encrypted gallery and personal relationship timeline, stored securely on your self-hosted instance.

• SOVEREIGN INFRASTRUCTURE
Run the entire backend yourself. The server stack is packaged as a lightweight Docker Compose project including Supabase (PostgreSQL + Auth), Coturn (WebRTC relay), and Node.js WebSocket signaling. Full self-hosting setup takes under 15 minutes on any VPS.
